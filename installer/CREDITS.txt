FIREBOY & WATERGIRL: THE FOREST TEMPLE ONLINE CO-OP - CREDITS
==============================================================

THE GAME: FIREBOY & WATERGIRL IN THE FOREST TEMPLE
--------------------------------------------------
Fireboy & Watergirl is created by Oslo Albet. All credit for the game, its
levels, art, music and design goes to its creators.

This is an unofficial, free fan mod. It is not made, endorsed or supported by
the game's developers. Please don't contact them about problems with this mod.
The mod contains no game files: the installer modifies your own copy of the
game, so you need to own it on Steam.

THE MOD
-------
  Online co-op mod:  Prompt|

TOOLS AND SERVICES
------------------
  PeerJS public signalling server (0.peerjs.com) - introduces the two games
    for join codes. https://peerjs.com
  WebRTC (built into the game's Electron/Chromium runtime) - the direct
    connection between the two PCs.
  Phaser and Box2D - the engine and physics the game is built with.
  Steam and Steamworks by Valve Corporation.

Thank you to the Fireboy & Watergirl team for a classic co-op game!
